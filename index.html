How Digital Self-Service Works

Five structured phases govern every interaction to maintain zero paper footprint and full organizational transparency.

1

Select Queue & Submit

Pick from 18 specialized department queues and complete the guided web intake form with attachments.

2

Instant Reference ID

Receive an encrypted tracking number (CICOD-SR-XXXX) with SMS & email delivery receipt.

3

Automated ECMS Triage

Ticket is ingested into CICOD ECMS workflows and routed to the accredited officer within minutes.

4

SLA-Backed Action

Live investigation and resolution proceed under guaranteed turnaround SLAs with real-time tracker updates.

5

Resolution & Archive

Formal signed resolution documents dispatched to applicant; audit history permanently archived in CICOD Drive.
